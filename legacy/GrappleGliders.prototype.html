A finished neon arcade climber: swing, reel, dash and collect charge cells before the Glitch catches you. Reach the breach gate at 1500 m to escape.

Controls

Left Mouse / touch hold — fire and hold grapple
Mouse Release — release rope and fling forward
A/D or arrows — air steer
W/S — reel rope in/out
Space / right click — dash toward cursor
P or Esc — pause

Rules

Blue cells restore dash and increase combo.
Green cells grant a one-hit shield.
Red sentries and the rising Glitch are lethal.
The gate opens when you reach 1500 m.
Tip: The strongest launches come from releasing the rope while your glider is moving upward and sideways.
